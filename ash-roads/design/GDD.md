Ash Roads – Game Design Document (Pre-Production)

⸻

1. Game Overview

Title: Ash Roads
Genre: Open-world action survival (3rd-person)
Platform(s): PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S
Engine: Unreal Engine 5 (Nanite + Lumen)
Target ESRB: M (Mature)

Tagline:

“The road keeps you alive. The world wants you dead.”

Core Pillars:
	1.	Survival on Wheels – Your bike is your lifeline.
	2.	Evolving Threats – Enemies learn your tactics.
	3.	Dynamic World – Weather, factions, and AI shift the balance daily.
	4.	Emotional Narrative – Gritty, personal, and morally gray.

⸻

2. Setting & Worldbuilding

Year: 2078
Location: Cascadia Expanse – fictional region inspired by the Pacific Northwest (forests, coastal towns, mountain passes, abandoned highways).
Cataclysm: “The Awakening” – biotech experiment fused parasitic fungus with human DNA, creating Hiveborn.

Factions:
	•	Ferrymen – Nomadic traders, neutral.
	•	Black Pines – Ex-military warlords.
	•	Hollow Choir – Religious cult worshipping Hiveborn.
	•	Lone Nomads – Survivalist NPCs, potential allies or enemies.

⸻

3. Narrative Synopsis

You play Calen Voss, an ex-smuggler searching for his kidnapped sister, Mira. Along the way, you’re joined by Ira, a damaged reconnaissance drone with a glitchy personality. The deeper you go into the Hiveborn territories, the more you uncover a conspiracy tying human greed to the Awakening — and the possibility of reversing it.

Story Arcs:
	•	Act 1: Learn survival basics, secure bike, find allies.
	•	Act 2: Factions war, Hiveborn evolve, safe zones collapse.
	•	Act 3: Take the fight into the heart of the Hiveborn “Nestlands.”

⸻

4. Core Gameplay Loop
	1.	Scout – Find resources, mark threats, discover routes.
	2.	Survive – Decide between combat or stealth.
	3.	Upgrade – Improve bike, weapons, and camp.
	4.	Control – Secure trade routes and safehouses.
	5.	Progress Story – Uncover truths and face evolving enemies.

⸻

5. Combat Systems
	•	Third-Person Shooting & Melee – Gritty, weighty animations with takedowns.
	•	Adaptive AI: Hiveborn react to your past tactics.
	•	Bike Combat: Sidearm shooting, melee strikes, trap deployment while riding.
	•	Crafting: On-the-fly upgrades & explosives.

Weapons:
	•	Firearms: Handguns, rifles, shotguns, crossbows.
	•	Melee: Axes, machetes, hammers (wear down over time).
	•	Throwables: Molotovs, pipe bombs, sonic decoys.

⸻

6. Enemy Types

Hiveborn:
	•	Drones – Fast swarmers.
	•	Mawcarvers – Stealth hunters that mimic human voices.
	•	Bursters – Suicide units filled with corrosive spores.
	•	Crownbeasts – Massive tanks with weak spots.
	•	Hive Mothers – Rare bosses, control hordes.

⸻

7. Progression
	•	Skill Trees:
	•	Survivor – Stealth, gathering, survival efficiency.
	•	Hunter – Damage, accuracy, weapon handling.
	•	Nomad – Bike handling, fuel efficiency, mounted combat.
	•	Bike Upgrades: Engine power, armor, sidecar storage, stealth mufflers, mounted weapons.
	•	Camp Upgrades: Recruit specialists (mechanic, medic, scout).

⸻

8. Missions & Activities

Main Missions: Story-driven, cinematic set pieces.
Side Missions: Rescue survivors, sabotage faction outposts, deliver supplies.
Dynamic Events: Horde migrations, faction wars, weather hazards.

Sample Mission: “Broken Choir”
	•	Objective: Infiltrate Hollow Choir’s radio tower and disrupt Hiveborn lure signals.
	•	Choices: Go loud and risk Hiveborn swarm, or stealth in and sabotage quietly.

⸻

9. World Systems
	•	Dynamic Weather: Snow slows movement, rain reduces noise, storms affect visibility.
	•	Day/Night Cycle: Hiveborn more aggressive at night.
	•	Faction Reputation: Allies can offer shelter, trade, or ambush you.

⸻

10. Technical Scope
	•	Estimated Development Time: 3.5 years.
	•	Team Size: ~120 developers.
	•	Budget: $50–$80 million.
	•	Target FPS: 60fps on current-gen consoles & PC.
